About Us

Learn Live Sound the Practical Way

RUACH Institute of Sound Engineering is dedicated to providing industry-oriented Live Sound Engineering training with a strong emphasis on practical learning.

Our curriculum is carefully designed to help students understand the complete workflow of professional live sound—from audio fundamentals and digital console operation to system tuning and live event execution.

Whether you're a beginner or already working in the audio industry, this course equips you with practical skills that can be applied immediately in real-world environments.

Course Modules

What You Will Learn

A complete, step-by-step syllabus designed to take you from the basics to real live sound operation.

Audio Fundamentals

  • Introduction to Live Sound Engineering
  • What is Sound?
  • How Sound is Produced
  • Frequency & Wavelength
  • Human Hearing Range
  • Audio Signal Flow
  • Balanced & Unbalanced Audio
  • Mic, Line & Speaker Levels

Microphones & Audio Connections

  • Audio Cables & Connectors
  • DI Box
  • Microphone Types
  • Polar Patterns
  • Phantom Power (48V)
  • Microphone Placement Techniques

Mixing Console Operation

  • Analog Mixing Console
  • Digital Console Operation
  • Channel Strip Functions
  • Gain Structure
  • Channel EQ
  • AUX & Monitor Mixing
  • Routing
  • DCA & Matrix
  • Scenes & Presets
  • Console Workflow

Audio Processing

  • Equalization (EQ)
  • High Pass & Low Pass Filters
  • Compressor
  • Gate
  • Limiter
  • Feedback Control
  • FX Processing
  • Reverb & Delay

Digital Mixing & Live Workflow

  • Digital Console Mixing
  • Live Mixing Techniques
  • Multitrack Recording
  • Professional Mixing Workflow
  • Monitor Mixing
  • Live Event Signal Management

DSP & System Configuration

  • DSP Fundamentals
  • DriveRack Configuration
  • Digital Crossovers
  • HPF / LPF
  • Phase & Polarity
  • Time Alignment
  • Signal Routing
  • System Optimization

Amplifiers & PA Systems

  • Crown Amplifier Configuration
  • Gain Settings
  • Stereo & Bridge Mode
  • Speaker Impedance Matching
  • Power Distribution
  • System Monitoring
  • Basic Troubleshooting

Practical Sessions

  • Console Setup
  • Live Sound System Setup
  • Stage Patch & Signal Flow
  • Digital Mixing Practice
  • DSP Configuration
  • System Tuning
  • Live Event Workflow
