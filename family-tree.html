A source-aware biblical genealogy

The family tree of Jesus

Ten recorded generations before Jesus, presented from Matthew’s genealogy, followed by the family relationships that the New Testament actually records. Dates are shown honestly: the Bible does not give exact birth dates for these generations.

Ten generations before Jesus

Reading left to right: Jesus → Joseph → Jacob and onward through Matthew 1:1–16. “Not recorded” means there is no reliable birth date in the biblical text.

Dates

Exact dates of birth are not supplied for Jesus’ ancestors. Rather than invent dates, this page labels them “Not recorded.” Jesus’ birth is traditionally estimated around 6–4 BC, but the exact date is unknown.

Which genealogy?

This visual follows the royal/legal line in Matthew 1:1–16: Joseph, Jacob, Matthan, Eleazar, Eliud, Achim, Zadok, Azor and Eliakim. Luke 3 presents a different genealogy and should be studied alongside this one.

Sources

Matthew 1:1–16, Luke 3:23–38, and Mark 6:3.

Jesus’ recorded family

The Bible does not record ten generations of descendants after Jesus. To keep this tree accurate, no names have been invented; these are the close family relationships named or described in the New Testament.

Important: Biblical genealogy is not a complete civil registry. Dates and some relationships are debated; this page distinguishes recorded facts from traditional estimates.
